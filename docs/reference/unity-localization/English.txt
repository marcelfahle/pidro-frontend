TID_HigherCardCanBe~A higher card can be used to take lower cards and points except for the 2 which always is safe point for the team who owns it.
TID_TheCorretPidro~The "correct" pidro, in this case 5 of Spades has higher ranking than the "wrong" pidro (5 of Clubs).
TID_DealerChip~This is the Dealer Chip.
TID_DealingStarts~Dealing starts from the left of Dealer.
TID_EachPlayersIsDealt~Each Player is dealt a total of nine (9) cards.
TID_ValidBitMustBe~A valid bid must be between six (6) and fourteen (14). By bidding you estimate how many minimum points you and your teammate at least will take in the round. If you don't want to make a bid, one can always pass. A bid must be higher than the current highest bid. The player who gives the highest bid may then choose suit to play in and starts the round.
TID_FinalDealing~FINAL DEALING
TID_WhenTheSuitToPlay~When the suit to play is chosen, unnecessary cards are thrown. Cards kept in hand are those of the suit that was chosen plus the other "PIDRO" and the exception is the dealer who always keeps his cards. Shortly thereafter the dealer deals the remaining cards in deck so that the players get 6 cards each in total. The dealer takes the remaining cards if there's any. If any player gets more than six cards of selected suit, the extra non-point cards will be automatically removed.
TID_TheHighestbidder~The highest bidder opens the round by putting a card of the current suit at the table (A), thereafter the other players clockwise put one card each (4,J,5). The team that has the highest card in the stick gets all points in the stick in question. In this scenario Antti has high card (A) and takes home A=1p, J=1p and 5=5p. Total 7 points to Antti and Tommy and Antti will continue next stick as he has High card (A).
TID_WhenAllCardsArePut~When all the cards are put on the table the points are calculated. If the bidding team got as much points as the bid or more, both teams' points are added to their respective existing points. If the bidding team gets fewer points than the bid, the bid is substracted from the bidding team's existing points. The other team's points are added to their existing points.
TID_WinnerOfTheGameisThe~Winner of the game is the team that first reaches 62 points or more. If both teams reach 62 points or more at the same time then the team having the highest bid in the final round wins.

TID_IfNoOneBids~- If no one bids the dealer is forced to make a bid.
TID_IfSomeoneBids~- If someone bids 14, the dealer is allowed to bet 14 as well and overtake the bid, but will risk losing 28 points if he fails to collect all the points i.e 14.
